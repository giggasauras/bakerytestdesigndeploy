Welcome to Defence Bakery!

Defence Bakery is a family-run Delhi bakery established in 1962. This website features its Panchsheel Enclave outlet at Holiday Club, near Gargi College.

Explore breads, cakes, cookies and pastries for a snack, a family meal or a celebration.
